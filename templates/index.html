About me

I am a professor in Department of Computer Science at Tianjin University and a member of TANK Lab, led by Prof. Keqiu Li. I received my Ph.D. degree from Networked Systems Lab at University of Southern California. I obtained my B.S. degree at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research interests include agentic systems, large language model (LLM) systems, and AI for Science (AI4Science). In collaboration with research institutions like IBM Watson, Samsung Research and Microsoft Research, I have published tens of papers at the leading conferences/journals, including Nature, ASPLOS, EuroSys, SoCC, Ubicomp, INFOCOM, IWQoS, SIGCOMM, TPDS and TC. My research is funded by NSFC Young Scientists Fund (Category B), and I have received honors such as U35 Outstanding Talent Award from Tianjin University and Best Paper Award from ACM SoCC'24. I have served as a program committee member for conferences such as INFOCOM, ISCA and HPCA, and am an associate editor for IEEE Transactions on Computers.

I have developed Twen.ai, a university Q&A large language model. Empowered by RAG techniques, Twen addresses daily questions from students and faculty in areas such as daily life, scholarship selection, further studies, etc. Twen was officially released in April 2024 and has since served thousands of requests each day. I am also developing Xiaotian, the official AI mentor that is available to all students at Tianjin University since September 2025.

Join our research group. I am looking for self-motivated students interested in building systems for large language models and AI for Science. If you are interested in working with me, please read this document.
